        Why it matters

        FoodLoop supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 12.3: halve food waste per person at retail and consumer level by 2030, and cut food losses along supply chains.

        Food that is thrown away also wastes the water, energy and money used to grow, cook and carry it.

        How it works

        1. A café, hall or student posts what is left and a pickup time.
        2. Someone nearby claims a portion.
        3. They collect it before the timer runs out.
        4. The impact counter adds one meal saved.
